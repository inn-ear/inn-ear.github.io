    How We Hear

    An interactive teaching tool. The simulations are simplified models meant to build intuition. They are not a hearing test and not medical advice. If you notice changes in your hearing, talk to an audiologist or doctor.

    Listen safely

    Use headphones at a comfortable level. Start quiet and turn up slowly. The site never plays louder than your device volume allows.
